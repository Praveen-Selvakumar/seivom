1. About LocalSevai

LocalSevai is a local service discovery platform designed to help customers find nearby service professionals such as plumbers, electricians, AC technicians, carpenters, painters and other home-service providers.

LocalSevai may provide these services through WhatsApp, our website and related digital services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use LocalSevai, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide.

Customer information

  • Name
  • WhatsApp phone number
  • Service requests
  • Area or location information
  • Messages sent to our WhatsApp service

Service provider information

  • Name or business name
  • Phone number
  • Services offered
  • Business/service area
  • Location information
  • Availability information

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to operate and improve LocalSevai and to provide the services requested by customers and service providers.

  • Find relevant local service providers.
  • Respond to customer requests.
  • Register and manage service providers.
  • Connect customers with service providers.
  • Maintain service availability and listings.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud and misuse.
  • Improve the performance and reliability of our services.

4. WhatsApp

LocalSevai may use the WhatsApp Business Platform to communicate with customers and service providers.

When you contact LocalSevai through WhatsApp, your messages and related information are processed through WhatsApp and Meta's services as applicable.

By communicating with LocalSevai through WhatsApp, you understand that your communication is also subject to WhatsApp's and Meta's applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Information Sharing

LocalSevai may share relevant information when necessary to provide the service requested by a customer.

For example, when a customer requests a plumber, information necessary to connect the customer with an appropriate plumber may be shared with that service provider.

We do not intend to sell personal information to unrelated third parties.

6. Location Information

Location information may be used to identify service providers near a customer's requested area.

You may choose to provide an area, locality or other location information when requesting a service.

Service providers may provide their service area or location so that customers can discover relevant local providers.

7. Data Storage

Information collected by LocalSevai may be stored using third-party infrastructure and cloud services used to operate our platform.

These services may include databases, hosting, authentication and messaging infrastructure.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled through LocalSevai.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and protect the security of our platform.

10. Your Choices

You may contact us to request information about personal information associated with your use of LocalSevai and, where applicable, request correction or deletion.

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legitimate business, security or legal purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

LocalSevai is intended for general users and is not designed specifically for children.

We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our services, technology or legal requirements change.

When we make changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
